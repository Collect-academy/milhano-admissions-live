MILHANO | V13 | RESPONDED + MEANINGFUL CONVERSATIONS
Date: 2026-08-14

============================================================
A. PIPELINE / GHL
============================================================

DO NOT create a Responded stage.
DO NOT create a Meaningful Conversation stage.

Stages represent current operational state / next action.
Interaction scorecards represent events that happened during the period.

Recommended early-stage interpretation:

Cliente potencial
No responde
Seguimiento
No fit
Lost / Sin continuidad
Fit (dashboard: Qualified / Fit)
School Tour agendado
...

Examples:
- Lead replies "call me tomorrow": Responded KPI + current stage Seguimiento.
- Lead provides relevant admissions info but there is still not enough to decide
  Fit vs No Fit: Meaningful Conversation KPI + current stage Seguimiento.
- Enough information and criteria met: Fit / Qualified.
- Enough information and criteria not met: No fit.

This avoids moving a card into a temporary interaction stage and then moving it
again just to represent its real current state.

B. V13 CASCADE
============================================================

New Leads
-> Number of Dials
-> Unique Contacted Leads
-> Responded
-> Meaningful Conversations
-> Qualified / Fit
-> ST Booked
-> ST Today
-> ST Attended
-> Trial Day Booked
-> Trial Day Showed
-> Closed

Unique Contacted Leads (system)
Distinct admissions leads with at least one observable human outbound attempt:
- outbound GHL call attempt, OR
- manual/countable outbound WhatsApp.

This is NOT the same as EOD Contactados. EOD Contactados is attempts and can
count multiple actions to the same lead.

Responded (system + reported reconciliation)
Distinct admissions leads with at least one observable response:
- inbound WhatsApp linked to an admissions opportunity, OR
- connected GHL call (outbound answered or inbound connected when available).

The EOD # Responses field is mapped to this scorecard as the advisor-reported
reference. The system count and reported count remain visible separately.

Meaningful Conversations (reported/manual-primary)
Distinct leads who provided admissions-relevant information, even if the
information is still insufficient to decide Fit vs No Fit. Can be WhatsApp or
phone. It is not inferred from duration or number of messages.

The Calls page keeps the old 3+ minute calculation, but V13 labels it explicitly
as Calls 3+ min (GHL), not Meaningful Conversations.
