CAPACITY NOTE
03A and 03B currently fetch at most the latest 100 messages/calls per run. If WhatsApp can exceed 100 messages between two scheduled polls, Configuration B can lose historical coverage between runs; use Configuration A (or a shorter 03A cadence) during high-volume campaigns. Calls are normally much lower-volume, so a slower call cadence is safer.
