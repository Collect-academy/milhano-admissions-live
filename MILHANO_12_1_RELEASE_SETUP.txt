SIMPLE EOD + QUALIFIED/FIT ALIAS + FOLLOW-UP SPLIT + BILINGUAL HELP

IMPORTANT CORRECTION FROM V12

Qualified and Fit are NOT separate stages.
They are the same Milhano business milestone.

GHL keeps the stage name:
Fit

The dashboard/EOD displays it as:
Qualified / Fit

DO NOT create a GHL stage named Qualified.

A. GHL PIPELINE CHANGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Previous early-stage structure:

V12.1 preferred structure:

No responde
Seguimiento

Lost / Sin continuidad
Fit
School Tour agendado
School Tour atendido
Pasadía agendada
Pasadía asistida
Retroalimentación
En evaluación
Inscripción en proceso
Inscrito

Recommended GHL steps:

1. Rename the existing stage:
   No responde / Seguimiento
   to:
   No responde

2. Create ONE new stage:
   Seguimiento

3. Place Seguimiento directly after No responde.

4. Do NOT create Qualified.

5. Existing opportunities that were in the combined stage will become
   No responde after the rename. Review the active ones and move to
   Seguimiento only those that really have a future contact action/callback.

Operational definitions:

No responde
- Contact attempts happened but no reply yet.
- Still active.
- NOT Lost.

Seguimiento
- There is a known next contact action or valid reason to retry later.
- Example: asked for a callback at another hour/day.
- Still active.

Lost / Sin continuidad
- Active pursuit is actually finished.
- Do not use simply because the requested callback time was inconvenient.

No fit
- Does not meet an admissions criterion.

Fit = Qualified
- Same business milestone.
- Dashboard label: Qualified / Fit.

Historical records named "No responde / Seguimiento" remain supported as
legacy so old history is not destroyed.
