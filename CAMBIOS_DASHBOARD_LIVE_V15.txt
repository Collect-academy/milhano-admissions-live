MILHANO DASHBOARD V15

1. EOD submitted records can now be corrected by the owning advisor.
2. Existing EOD values are pre-filled during edits.
3. EOD values are explicitly FINAL TOTALS, never +delta adjustments.
4. Every EOD ? includes the replace-not-add instruction.
5. Submitted EODs show a dedicated Update EOD action.
6. Validated EODs remain admin-only; editing a validated EOD invalidates the old validation.
7. New field-level EOD audit table stores before -> after changes.
8. New Logs view is accessible to every authenticated dashboard user.
9. Logs show actor, advisor, date, changed fields, old/new values, note changes and timestamp using simple wording.
10. Reconciliation now has Download CSV for a GHL Tracking Gap report.
11. Gap report includes GHL/System, Reported, Verified Outside GHL, unresolved gap, estimated not captured in GHL and GHL capture rate.
12. Manual-by-design KPIs are labeled to avoid interpreting expected manual metrics as tracking failures.
13. No new n8n workflows or scheduled executions are introduced in V15.
