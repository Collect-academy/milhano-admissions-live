MILHANO | V14 | HISTORICAL EOD + MANUAL MEANINGFUL

WHAT V14 ADDS

1. "Subir EOD anterior" / "Add previous EOD" on the EOD page.
2. Advisor selects a prior date and gets their own EOD for that date.
3. Admin selects both date and advisor.
4. Existing (advisor, date) submissions are reopened instead of duplicated.
5. Submitted EODs remain read-only for advisors.
6. Missing/draft historical EODs refresh their system evidence before opening. Already submitted/validated EODs are opened without silently refreshing their closed snapshot.
7. Meaningful Conversations stays in the simple EOD as a manual KPI.
8. No n8n workflow is created for Meaningful Conversations.

MEANINGFUL CONVERSATION DEFINITION
Unique lead who provided admissions-relevant information during the EOD window, even if the information is still insufficient to determine Fit vs No Fit. It can happen by WhatsApp or phone.
